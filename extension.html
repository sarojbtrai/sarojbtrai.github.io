How It Works

Get started in 4 simple steps

1

Install the Extension

Click the "Install for Firefox" button above to add the extension to your browser. It will appear in your toolbar with a purple icon.

2

Navigate to a Faucet

Visit any supported crypto faucet website. The compact rotator bar will automatically appear on your screen, showing your available faucet intervals.

3

Rotate Between Faucets

Use the arrow buttons (◀ ▶) to quickly navigate between faucets in your 5-minute or 1-hour lists. The extension tracks which site you're on and jumps to the next/previous one instantly.

4

Customize Your Experience

Drag the rotator to your preferred screen location using the handle (⋮⋮). Click the minimize button (−) to collapse it when done. Your settings are automatically saved.

Your Privacy Matters

Built with privacy-first principles

🔒 Zero Data Collection

This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. We don't track your browsing history, we don't sell your information, and we don't require any permissions beyond what's necessary for the extension to function.

The only data saved is your UI preferences (position, collapsed state) stored locally in your browser. The only external request is to fetch the public faucet configuration from our server.
